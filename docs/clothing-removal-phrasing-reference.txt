- Concrete tempo beats > vague pacing: "to the rhythm of the music", a slow

NOTES ON CURRENT CODE MODEL (src/data/clothing.js)
================================================================================
- removalVerb[item]: the mechanical action template, e.g.
    Bra      -> "unclasp {item} and slide the straps down"
    Shirt    -> "unbutton {item} and slide it off the shoulders"
    Belt     -> "unbuckle {item}"
    Pants    -> "slide {item} down over the hips"
    Stockings-> "roll {item} down the legs"
  {item} renders as "your X" (self) or "Sam's X" (partner).
- removalManners[]: flourish appended after the verb. Each manner has:
    lead       : adverb before the verb ('' or 'slowly')
    trail      : clause appended after a comma (the phrase)
    allow      : category gate (null = any), categories from garmentCategory()
    allowItems : exact item-name gate (e.g. ['Belt'], ['Shirt','Cardigan'])
    mode       : 'both' | 'partner' | 'self'
- Keep verbs mechanical and manners as presence/pacing so they don't repeat the
  same action (avoid "lift ... up and off" + "lift it up and off").
