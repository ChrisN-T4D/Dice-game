Anatomy map — illustration base + interactive zones

The AI illustration is the base layer; clickable zones are overlaid in normalized coordinates and tinted live from the real zone profiles. Click a zone for detail, switch the metric to re-tint. Scroll or double-click to zoom into tight clusters (glans/frenulum, clitoris/hood); drag to pan, and use the corner buttons to reset. Turn on Calibrate to drag zones onto the art (orange handle = resize, green handle = rotate/tilt) and Export the coordinates.
